The ConDefects programs in this directory are adapted from submissions to AtCoder distributed by Yonghao Wu, Zheng Li, Jie M. Zhang and Yong Liu in [ConDefects](https://github.com/appmlk/ConDefects). The individual submission IDs, source paths and pinned revision are in [manifest.json](manifest.json). We converted stdin/stdout scripts to functions, added our own tests, and produced renamed copies. The QuixBugs programs and test cases come from the replication package's MIT-licensed snapshot. The renamed copies preserve the same license. See the [QuixBugs license](../quixbugs/QUIXBUGS_LICENSE) and [manifest](../quixbugs/manifest.json).
