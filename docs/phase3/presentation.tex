\begin{frame}{What the experiment establishes}
The held-out tests can expose a shared mistake that survives both fixing and review. The paired task counts measure when extra roles change the result.\par\vspace{.35cm}
A published GPT-o1 study repaired all 40 QuixBugs programs under its own protocol. This study establishes no improvement over that result.\par\vspace{.35cm}
